# Voice Selection Guide

Choosing the right voice pairing for your podcast style.

## Available Voices

### Male Voices

| Voice | Character | Best For |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Puck** | Conversational, friendly | Casual chat host, interview guest, explainer host |
| **Charon** | Deep, authoritative | Scientific expert, debate moderator, news anchor |
| **Fenrir** | Energetic, dynamic | Tech deep-dive enthusiast, storytelling narrator |
| **Orus** | Calm, measured | Executive briefing, scientific analysis, debate guest |

### Female Voices

| Voice | Character | Best For |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Kore** | Neutral, professional | News briefing host, technical deep-dive host |
| **Aoede** | Warm, melodic | Interview host, storytelling narrator, explainer host |
| **Leda** | Clear, articulate | Scientific presenter, executive briefing, debate |
| **Zephyr** | Light, upbeat | Casual chat, topic explainer, lighter interview |

## Single Speaker Recommendations

| Style | Voice | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| News briefing | Kore or Charon | Professional and authoritative |
| Topic explainer | Aoede or Puck | Warm and approachable |
| Storytelling | Fenrir or Aoede | Dynamic or melodic narration |
| Executive briefing | Leda or Orus | Clear and measured |

## Tips

- **Contrast matters:** Pair voices with different energy levels (e.g., calm host + energetic guest) for more engaging dialogue
- **Match the show brand:** A casual show with Charon as host will feel tonally mismatched
- **Test with short content first:** Generate a SHORT podcast to audition voice pairings before committing to LONG content
- **Gender isn't prescriptive:** Any voice works for any role — choose by character, not gender
